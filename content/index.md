I am currently a second-year Ph.D. candidate at the **[Computer Systems Research Group](https://github.com/pkusys)** in the **School of Computer Science at Peking University**, advised by Prof. [Xin Jin](https://xinjin.github.io/). Prior to that, I received my B.S. in Computer Science, also from Peking University. 

My research interests include system and algorithm design for generative AI (primarily LLMs), formal verification, and now their [[Verge|potential crossover]]. I am also a bit of a programming language nerd.

Outside of my academic life, I like music! I am a self-taught guitar player and enjoy a lot of j-rock / j-pop / jazz / fusion music.
